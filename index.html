A little about us

A home for
curious minds.

More than a building full of books, Greenwood is a neighbourhood space built around the simple idea that knowledge belongs to everyone. For over four decades, our doors have welcomed readers, students, families and lifelong learners.

Find a quiet corner to focus, discover a new author, bring the little ones for story time or spend an afternoon exploring something you have always wanted to understand. There is room here for every kind of curiosity.

Our shelves bring together familiar favourites and fresh perspectives, while our digital resources make learning easier to access. Our team is always happy to help you find your next read.

Come as you are, stay as long as you like, and make Greenwood a part of your everyday.
